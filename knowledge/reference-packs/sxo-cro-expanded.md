## Intent to action match

Search intent should determine the page's next action. Informational pages may need guides, comparison pages need proof and alternatives, and transactional pages need trust, clarity, friction reduction, and visible calls to action.

<a id="seo-cro-measurement"></a>
## SEO CRO measurement

Measure both search progress and business action. A ranking gain without qualified action is incomplete; a conversion lift without search evidence may belong to CRO rather than SEO.
