<a id="sro-evidence-pipeline"></a>
## SRO evidence pipeline

Evaluate AI-search selection readiness as a staged evidence chain: query/prompt set, platform observations,
SERP context, page extraction, site/entity context, and final recommendation. Do not turn one platform's
citation into a universal visibility claim.

<a id="aeo-readiness-checks"></a>
## AEO readiness checks

Useful AEO checks include direct-answer placement, heading hierarchy, extractable summaries, structured
data eligibility, entity clarity, and public crawl/index eligibility. `llms.txt` can be recorded as
governance context, but it is not a Google Search ranking or citation factor.

<a id="ai-visibility-observation-contract"></a>
## AI visibility observation contract

Every AI visibility observation must record model or search surface, prompt/query, date, locale or market,
brand/page citation state, competitor citation state, linked or unlinked mention state, and evidence limits.

<a id="citation-opportunity-ethics"></a>
## Citation opportunity ethics

Citation opportunities should favor authoritative pages, source-worthy assets, expert proof, and legitimate
partnerships. Do not recommend fabricated mentions, fake citations, undisclosed sponsorship, or manipulative
third-party placements.
