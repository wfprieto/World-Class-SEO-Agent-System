## Local eligibility

Local optimization must start with real-world eligibility: business model, service area, address rules, hours, categories, and user-facing service evidence. Do not recommend fake locations, fake reviews, or keyword-stuffed names.

<a id="local-page-proof"></a>
## Local page proof

Local landing pages need local proof: services offered in the market, service-area details, reviews or case examples when allowed, staff or location facts, route/context detail, and clear conversion paths.
