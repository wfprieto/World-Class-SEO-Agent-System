## Template uniqueness

Programmatic and ecommerce pages need unique, useful content at scale: real inventory, specs, pricing context, availability, comparisons, reviews where valid, and page-specific answers.

<a id="merchant-data-consistency"></a>
## Merchant data consistency

Product page claims, structured data, feeds, images, availability, shipping, returns, and pricing should agree. Conflicts reduce trust and create reporting noise.
