<a id="information-gain"></a>
## Information gain

Public content should add original evidence, firsthand experience, proprietary examples, clearer synthesis, better utility, or a sharper decision aid. Generic summaries without proof are low-value even when grammatically polished.

<a id="public-copy-quality"></a>
## Public copy quality

Any visitor-visible text must be specific, plain, defensible, and useful. Remove unsupported superlatives, vague claims, fake urgency, filler intros, and language that sounds generated instead of observed.
