## Hreflang cluster integrity

Every localized URL cluster needs reciprocal alternates, valid language or region codes, canonical self-consistency, and a deliberate x-default decision. Missing return links can invalidate otherwise well-intended targeting.

<a id="localization-quality"></a>
## Localization quality

Localized SEO is not word-for-word translation. Confirm market vocabulary, legal terms, units, currency, cultural context, local proof, and regional search-engine requirements.
