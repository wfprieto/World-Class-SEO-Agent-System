## AI citation readiness

AI-search readiness depends on crawlable, extractable, source-backed, entity-clear content. No file, tag, or prompt guarantees citation. Record the search surface, prompt/query, date, location, and observed citation behavior.

<a id="entity-answer-structure"></a>
## Entity answer structure

Pages should identify the entity, answer core questions directly, show evidence, expose relationships, and avoid burying definitions inside vague marketing copy.
