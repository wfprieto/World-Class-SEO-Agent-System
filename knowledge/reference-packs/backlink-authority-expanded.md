## Earned authority

The strongest authority signals come from useful assets, original data, expert commentary, tools, partnerships, and legitimate editorial references. Avoid buying links, artificial networks, fake citations, or undisclosed sponsorship.

<a id="defensive-link-review"></a>
## Defensive link review

Treat suspicious links as evidence to investigate, not automatic disavow targets. Escalate only when patterns indicate manipulation, malware, hacked pages, or serious brand/security risk.
